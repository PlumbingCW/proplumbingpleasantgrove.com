Why faucets leak

The usual causes of a drip

Most faucets drip because an internal seal wore out. Compression faucets use rubber washers, cartridge faucets use a sealed insert and ceramic disc models use a disc pair. The part is small, but picking the right one for the brand matters.

A leak at the base of the spout points to worn O-rings. A leak at the handle points to the cartridge or packing. A leak from the supply connection under the sink means a loose or worn line.

Fixes

Problems we repair beyond drips

Faucet work covers more than leaks. These are the other common calls.

  • Low flow from a clogged aerator or mineral buildup.
  • Hot and cold swapped or a mixing valve that drifts.
  • A loose faucet that wobbles on the sink.
  • A sprayer or pull-down hose that leaks or will not retract.
  • A shower valve that will not shut off or runs hot and cold.
  • An outdoor spigot that drips or sprays inside the wall.
Outdoor taps

Frost-proof spigots and winter damage

Outdoor spigots can freeze if a hose is left attached through winter, even on frost-proof models. The split often shows up in spring when the tap is turned on and water appears inside the wall. Disconnect hoses before the first hard freeze and ask a plumber to check any spigot that drips from the handle.

A spigot leak inside the wall can rot framing, so check the interior side if the tap sprays when it is on.

Local water

Hard water and faucet life

Utah County water carries dissolved minerals that leave crusty scale on aerators, cartridges and shower heads. Scale shortens cartridge life and cuts flow. Vinegar soaks help on aerators, and a softener or filter can slow scale across the house.

When to replace

If a faucet is more than fifteen years old, the finish is failing or parts are hard to find, replacement is often the better value. We install faucets you supply or help you pick one that fits the sink.

Standards

Flow ratings and the WaterSense label

The U.S. EPA WaterSense label for bathroom faucets means a flow of 1.5 gallons per minute or less, with performance checked by test. If you replace a faucet, a labeled model saves water without a weak stream, and an aerator swap on an existing faucet can do part of the job.
