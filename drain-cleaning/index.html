Emergency

When a clogged drain is an emergency

A single slow sink can wait for a day visit. A toilet that will not flush, water backing up into a tub when you run the washing machine, or sewage smell with gurgling in several drains is an emergency. Those signs point to the main line, and every flush adds to the mess.

  1. Stop using sinks, tubs, dishwasher and washing machine until a plumber has looked.
  2. Do not run a chemical drain opener into standing water, because it can burn skin and damage pipe.
  3. Lift rugs and valuables away from the lowest drain, usually a basement floor drain or shower.
  4. Call the 24/7 line and say which fixtures bubble or overflow first.
Kitchen sinks

Why kitchen sinks clog differently

Kitchen drains clog from grease, food starch and soap, which cool into a paste a few feet from the sink. Pouring boiling water helps briefly and then the paste returns. A plumber uses a cable to cut the blockage and often follows with a jetting pass to scour the pipe wall.

If a garbage disposal sits above the clog, we check it before cabling, since a jammed disposal can look like a drain problem. See garbage disposal repair if the unit hums but will not turn.

Symptoms

Signs the problem is bigger than one sink

A single fixture clogs for local reasons. Several fixtures clogging together points to a shared line.

  • Water backs up into a tub or shower when the toilet is flushed.
  • The lowest drain in the house, often a basement floor drain, bubbles or fills.
  • Multiple fixtures drain slowly at the same time.
  • Gurgling sounds from drains after the washing machine drains.
  • A sewer smell inside, near the floor drain or under a sink.
Methods

We pick the tool by clog type and pipe condition. This is how the common methods compare.

A plumber should cable only as far as the pipe condition allows. Brittle cast iron and cracked clay need a gentler approach.

Drain lines

Drain line leaks and repeat clogs

A drain that clears and then clogs again within weeks often has a cause you cannot see. Roots, a bellied section, or a crack that lets soil in will rebuild a blockage over and over. Callers also notice damp spots or a musty smell near a drain line, which can mean the pipe itself is leaking.

We use a camera when a clog repeats or when the symptoms do not match a simple blockage. If it shows a damaged section, sewer line repair is the fix, and cleaning alone will not hold.

Insurance note

Homeowner policies often exclude backups unless you carry a water backup endorsement. Check yours, and photograph any damage before you clean up.

Code

Why cleanouts matter

Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) statewide, with state amendments, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. The model code calls for cleanouts at key points in a drain system, which is why a house with an accessible cleanout is quicker and cheaper to clear than one without. If yours is buried or missing, ask us about adding one during the next visit.
