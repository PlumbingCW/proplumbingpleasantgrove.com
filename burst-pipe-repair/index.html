Emergency

What to do in the first five minutes

Speed matters more than anything else. Water through a ceiling or wall soaks insulation and drywall within minutes, and electrical circuits nearby become a hazard.

  1. Shut off the main water valve. It is usually near the water heater, in a basement or where the line enters the house.
  2. Open a faucet at the lowest level to drain the pressure from the lines.
  3. Switch off power to wet areas at the breaker only if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  4. Move valuables out of the water path and photograph the damage for your insurer.
  5. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher where the water is coming from.
Cold weather

Why pipes burst in Pleasant Grove

Pleasant Grove winters bring hard freezes, and pipes in exterior walls, crawl spaces and unheated garages are the usual victims. Water expands as it freezes, and the pipe splits where ice blocks the flow. The leak often shows only after the ice thaws, which is why a burst can be discovered hours after the freeze.

Pressure also matters. Homes at higher elevation on the east bench can have high street pressure, and a pressure regulator that has failed can stress old fittings until one lets go.

Causes

Common causes and the fix

Burst pipes follow a few patterns. Knowing the cause shapes the repair.

Insurance often covers sudden burst damage but not slow leaks, so document the timing and photograph everything.

Aftercare

What to do once the water is off

Stopping the flow is only the start. These steps limit damage while repairs happen.

  • Run towels, a wet vac and fans to pull standing water out fast.
  • Open cabinet doors and lift wet baseboards so walls can dry.
  • Keep photos and a list of damaged items for the insurer.
  • Do not turn the water back on until the plumber confirms the repair.
  • Call a water removal company for any soaked drywall or flooring.
Repair process

How we repair a burst pipe

The plumber isolates the failed section, cuts out the damaged pipe and replaces it with new copper, PEX or PVC depending on the line. We pressure test before closing anything up. If a pipe burst in a wall, we open the smallest access we can and leave the patch easy for your drywall contractor.

We also look at the rest of the run. If one section froze, neighbors in the same cold wall often did too, and a quick check can prevent a second call next week.

Insurance and prevention

Many policies cover sudden bursts but not damage that builds slowly. Keep the failed pipe section if you can, since an adjuster may want to see it. Insulating exposed pipes and keeping the heat on in cold snaps prevents most freezes.

Pressure

High pressure breaks pipes, and the code addresses it

The model plumbing code calls for a pressure reducing valve when static water pressure goes above 80 psi. Utah applies the IPC statewide with amendments. If your pipes bang or a supply line has failed more than once, ask the plumber to put a gauge on a hose bib before any repair is closed up.
