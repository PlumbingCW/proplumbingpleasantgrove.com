The method

What hydro jetting actually does

A hydro jetter pushes a hose with a forward and backward spraying nozzle through the line. The backward jets pull the hose forward while they scour the pipe wall. Grease clears, roots are cut at their ends and scale breaks into pieces that wash out of the cleanout.

A cable cuts a hole through a blockage the size of the cutter. Jetting restores the full diameter, which is why callers who were told their line had been snaked repeatedly notice the difference. Many calls we log start with a plumber who snaked a line that then clogged again within weeks.

Emergency

Sewer backup: what to do first

If sewage is coming up through a floor drain or a tub, treat it as an emergency. Hydro jetting is often the fastest way to open a full main line that a cable could not clear.

  1. Stop all water use and flushing in the house.
  2. Keep people and pets out of the affected room and avoid contact with the water.
  3. Turn off power to a wet basement if you can do so without stepping in water.
  4. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher what has already been tried.
Good candidates

Problems jetting solves well

These are the situations where a jetter is usually worth the extra cost.

  • A kitchen drain line that clogs every few weeks from grease.
  • A main line with partial root growth that returns every year.
  • Scale buildup in older cast iron that narrows the pipe.
  • A commercial line from a restaurant or shop with heavy grease loads.
  • A line where a cable got stuck or came back clean but water still moves slowly.
Local conditions
