Sizing

Pick the right size before you pick a brand

Sizing follows your peak hour demand, not the number of bedrooms. A 40 gallon tank works for one or two people, a 50 gallon unit suits most families of four, and larger homes with soaking tubs often want 65 gallons or a tankless setup. First hour rating on the yellow label matters more than the nominal gallons.

We ask how many showers run at the same time, whether you have a large tub, and whether a dishwasher and laundry run during mornings. Those answers set the size.

The visit

How a scheduled installation runs

A normal tank installation follows five stages, and each stage has a check you can watch.

1

Site check

We confirm gas line size, vent path, floor drain and clearance.

2

Shutoff and drain

Water and fuel are shut off and the old tank is drained.

3

Set and connect

New unit goes on a pan, with flex connectors, shutoffs and a relief valve line.

4

Fuel and vent

Gas lines get a leak test and vents are checked for draw.

5

Fill and test

Tank is filled, purged, lit and checked for leaks and temperature.

Code items

Details a clean install should include

These are the small items that separate a clean install from a rushed one.

  • A pressure relief valve with a discharge pipe that ends where water can be seen, not into a wall.
  • An expansion tank when your plumbing is a closed system with a pressure regulator or backflow device.
  • Dielectric unions or flexible connectors where copper meets steel.
  • A drain pan and, where possible, a path for water to leave the room if the tank fails.
  • Combustion air and a vent pipe sloped and sealed to the manufacturer's instructions.

Local

Permits, inspections and utility notes in Pleasant Grove

Fuel changes, new gas runs and new venting often need a permit and inspection. Permit rules vary by job, so ask the Pleasant Grove building department what yours requires. Gas service in Utah comes from Enbridge Gas Utah, and any suspected leak should go to 911 and Enbridge at 1-800-767-1689 first.

Garage installs also deserve a look at freeze exposure and vehicle impact. Bollards or a raised platform are inexpensive protection, and a plumber can add them during the install visit.

Code

What an inspector looks at on a new install

Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) statewide, with state amendments, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. An inspector commonly checks the relief valve and its discharge, the venting on a gas unit, thermal expansion control where the system is closed and whether the appliance was installed to the maker's instructions. Permit rules vary, so ask the Pleasant Grove building department.
