Hot water problems

When a tank leaks at the base or runs out of life, water heater replacement gets the house back on hot water the same day when stock allows. If the unit only has a failed part or a tripped element, water heater repair is the cheaper first stop.

Planned upgrades go through water heater installation, and homeowners weighing an on-demand unit can read about a tankless water heater in Utah County water before they spend.

Drains, sewers and backups

A slow sink or a backed-up tub starts with drain cleaning, and a line that keeps clogging usually needs hydro jetting to scour out what a cable leaves behind.

When every drain in the house misbehaves together, sewer line cleaning clears the main, a sewer inspection puts a camera on the cause, and sewer line repair fixes a crack, a belly or a root intrusion.

Leaks, pipes and water control

Water where it should not be is the job of burst pipe repair when a line has failed and leak detection when you only know the meter keeps spinning. Trouble between the street and your wall belongs to water line repair.

Aging pipe is the long game, so repiping is worth pricing for galvanized or pinholed copper. Basement water gets sump pump repair first and basement waterproofing when the cause is drainage, not a failed pump.

Fixtures, installs and commercial calls

Fixtures wear out one at a time: toilet repair, faucet repair and garbage disposal repair are the most common visits, and plumbing installation covers new fixtures and remodel rough-ins.

If your tap water leaves scale or spots, water softener and filtration work starts with a tap test. Shops and offices can call about commercial plumbing, including after-hours problems.
