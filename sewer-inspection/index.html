The basics

What a camera inspection tells you

The camera head has lights and a self-leveling lens that stays upright as it moves. The plumber pushes it from a cleanout toward the city connection while a monitor shows the pipe. Distance counters mark where each defect sits, so a repair can be planned precisely.

You get the video and a plain-language summary. The summary names material, defect types and your best next step, whether that is nothing, a cleaning or a repair.

When to schedule

Situations that call for a camera look

Most inspections are planned around one of these events.

  • Before buying a home, especially one with large trees or older clay and cast iron lines.
  • Before a basement remodel or bathroom addition that adds drain load.
  • After two or more clogs in a year with no obvious cause.
  • After a root intrusion cleaning, to see if a repair is needed.
  • When you suspect a bellied section or a shifted joint after settling or heavy rain.

Home buyers

Inspections for buyers in Pleasant Grove

Many Pleasant Grove sales involve homes with mature landscaping, and tree roots are a common cause of lateral damage. A home inspection usually does not include the underground line. A camera look during the inspection period can save a buyer from an unexpected repair after closing.

If the camera finds a problem, you can ask for a repair, a credit or a price change, and the video is clear evidence for that conversation. Our blog on spotting sewer line trouble lists the signs sellers sometimes miss.

Local context

Utilities and the line you can inspect

Pleasant Grove's sewer mains are managed by the city and flow to treatment by the Timpanogos Special Service District. The camera inspects your private lateral from the cleanout toward the street connection. If it finds a problem beyond your property line, ask Pleasant Grove Public Works to look at the main.

Insurers rarely pay for an inspection that is not tied to a covered loss. Some home warranties include sewer coverage, so check your plan before you book.
