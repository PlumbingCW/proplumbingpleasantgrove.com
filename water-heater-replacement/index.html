When a leaking water heater is an emergency

Water pooling around the tank, rust-colored drips or a tank that rumbles and then goes cold all point to a failing unit. A leak from the tank body cannot be patched, because the steel liner has corroded through. Replacement is the only repair, and it is best done before the leak soaks drywall or carpet.

  1. Close the cold water inlet valve on top of the heater by turning the handle a quarter turn.
  2. Switch the breaker off for an electric unit, or set a gas unit's control to pilot or off.
  3. Drain a few gallons from the hose bib at the bottom only if the water is cool and a drain is nearby.
  4. Move boxes and stored items away from the base, then call (385) 488-1892 and describe the leak.
Symptoms

Signs the tank is past saving

Callers describe the same handful of symptoms before a tank gives out. Any two of these together usually means replacement will cost less than another repair.

  • Water on the floor that keeps returning after you wipe it up, especially at the base.
  • Hot water that runs out in minutes or never gets past lukewarm.
  • Rusty or brown water from hot taps only, while cold taps run clear.
  • Popping or rumbling as the tank heats, a sign of hardened sediment on the bottom.
  • A unit older than about 10 to 12 years, or one with a recall-era brand you cannot find parts for.
Local water

What Utah County water does to a tank

The City of Pleasant Grove supplies culinary water from springs and wells, and its 2023 water quality report lists total dissolved solids between 164 and 396 parts per million. Dissolved minerals settle as scale on the tank floor and heating elements. The report does not give a hardness number, so a cheap home test strip tells you more about your own tap.

Scale makes tanks noisy, slows recovery and shortens life. When we replace a unit we can add a dielectric union, a new expansion tank where the plumbing is closed and a flush schedule so the next heater lasts longer.

Planning ahead

What happens during the swap

A typical replacement takes a few hours. The plumber shuts off supply, drains the old tank, disconnects water and gas or power, sets the new unit on a pan or drain-safe floor, reconnects with new flex lines and checks the temperature and pressure relief valve discharge. Gas connections are leak tested before the pilot is lit.

We then fill the tank, purge the lines, set the thermostat near 120 degrees Fahrenheit and show you where the shutoffs are. The old heater leaves with us, so you are not left with a 100 pound tank in the garage.

What insurance may pay

Homeowner policies usually cover sudden water damage from a failed tank but not the cost of the heater itself or slow, long-term seepage. Keep photos and the old tank label for your claim.

Ready for a real number? Call (385) 488-1892 and describe the unit. If you would rather plan the swap, water heater installation can be scheduled at a calmer hour.

Code

Code rules that shape a replacement

Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) statewide, with state amendments, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. That code is why a new heater needs a temperature and pressure relief valve with a full-size discharge pipe that is never capped or plugged. Gas venting and permits are checked locally, so ask the Pleasant Grove building department what your job needs.
