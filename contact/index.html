Be ready

What helps the dispatcher most

You do not need technical words. Plain description is enough, and these details speed up the call.

  • The street address and the nearest cross street or the name of your subdivision.
  • What you see, hear or smell, and when it started.
  • Whether you have already found and shut off the main water valve.
  • Whether the problem is one fixture, one floor or the whole house.
