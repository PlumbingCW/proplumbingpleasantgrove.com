What we install

Fixtures and systems we install

Plumbing installation covers a wide range of projects. We set toilets, vanities and kitchen sinks, install tubs and showers, run laundry and utility lines, and connect dishwashers, refrigerators and ice makers. We can add a bathroom in a basement or reroute lines for a kitchen remodel.

Each job starts with a look at the existing supply, drain and vent lines, because new fixtures must tie into them correctly. A drain with the wrong slope or a missing vent will gurgle and clog, and it fails inspection.

Planning

Before you start a remodel

Plumbing is easiest to get right at the start. These steps save money and delays.

  • Decide fixture locations before walls close up.
  • Check that the main drain has enough capacity and fall for new fixtures.
  • Confirm the venting plan with your plumber, since a wrong vent causes gurgling.
  • Ask about a cleanout for any new line.
  • Schedule rough in and inspection before drywall.
Basements

Basement bathrooms in Pleasant Grove homes

Many Pleasant Grove homes have basements, and finishing one often means adding a bathroom. Below grade, the drain sits lower than the main sewer line, so waste may need to be lifted by an ejector pump or the slab cut for a gravity line. A plumber can show which suits your house.

Check for moisture first. A basement that seeps needs attention before finishing, and our page on basement waterproofing explains the options.

Local rules

Permits and licensed work

Utah requires plumbing work to be done by licensed contractors in most cases, and the state Division of Professional Licensing lets you check a license. Permits and inspections protect you and your resale value. Ask the contractor you hire to confirm what Pleasant Grove requires.

Code

Venting and trap rules for new fixtures

Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) statewide, with state amendments, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. New fixtures must be trapped and vented, and the distance from a trap to its vent is limited. That is why moving a sink across a room is not a casual job, and why a plumber lays out the drain before any wall is closed.
