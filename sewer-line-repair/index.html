Emergency

When a sewer problem cannot wait

Sewage coming up a floor drain, multiple fixtures backing up and a sewer smell indoors all say the lateral line is blocked or broken. This is a health and property emergency, because wastewater carries bacteria and spreads fast on a basement floor.

  1. Stop using water and flushing, including the washer and dishwasher.
  2. Keep people and pets away and wear boots and gloves if you must enter the wet area.
  3. Photograph the damage for insurance before cleanup.
  4. Call the 24/7 line and tell the dispatcher how many fixtures are affected.
Symptoms

Signs of a damaged sewer line

Damage often shows up slowly before it becomes an emergency. These signs are worth a camera look.

  • Slow drains throughout the house that return after cleaning.
  • Gurgling toilets or bubbles in a tub when another fixture drains.
  • Wet or unusually green patches over the lateral in a yard.
  • Sewer odor near the basement floor drain or outside by the cleanout.
  • Cracks, sinkholes or sagging soil along the line path.
Local conditions

What stresses sewer lines in Pleasant Grove

Pleasant Grove sits at the base of the Wasatch Front, and its soils shift with seasonal moisture and freeze and thaw cycles. Roots from the mature trees that give the city its nickname find hairline cracks and joints, then widen them. Older neighborhoods near the historic downtown have clay and cast iron laterals that crack with age.

City mains are the responsibility of the city and wastewater is treated by the Timpanogos Special Service District. Your lateral from the house to the main is generally your responsibility, and Pleasant Grove Public Works can tell you where the city line starts.

Planning

What a repair visit looks like

The plumber clears the line enough to run a camera, records the damage and marks its distance from the cleanout. You see the footage and hear the options, along with the price for each. If a dig is needed, we handle the locate call, protect landscaping where possible and backfill the trench.

Lining and spot repairs can often be done the same day or the next day, depending on the line condition. Permits may apply, so ask the Pleasant Grove building department about your job.

Insurance and the city

Standard homeowner policies often exclude root or wear damage to a lateral, although some offer sewer line endorsements. Check yours, and ask the city whether any portion belongs to a public main.

Call (385) 488-1892 and describe the symptoms. A first visit may start with hydro jetting to open the line so the camera can see.

Code

Rules a sewer repair must follow

Utah applies the International Plumbing Code (IPC) statewide, with state amendments, under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. Pipe material, slope and cleanout placement are part of what a repair must meet, and an excavation in Utah starts with a Blue Stakes 811 call. Ask the Pleasant Grove building department whether your repair needs a permit and inspection.
