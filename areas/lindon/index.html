If you have a burst pipe or active leak in Lindon

These steps take a few minutes and can save a floor. Do them while you wait for the plumber to reach you.

  1. Close the main water valve, usually near the water heater or where the line enters the house.
  2. Open a tap on the lowest level to drain pressure from the pipes.
  3. Turn off power to wet rooms at the breaker if the panel is dry and you can reach it safely.
  4. Clear the area of rugs and stored boxes.
  5. Call (385) 488-1892 and give the street and nearest cross road.
What Lindon calls about

The problems Lindon homes call about most

Lindon is a small city of about 11,400 residents at the 2020 census, and it mixes older farm-era homes with newer subdivisions and some light industrial land along its southern and western edges. That mix shapes the calls. Older properties raise water heater failures, galvanized lines and clay sewer laterals. Newer homes lead with fixture leaks and drain clogs.

Water heater failure tops the list in many calls, followed by clogged drains and slow leaks that show up on a water bill. A tank more than ten years old is a candidate for water heater replacement, and a drain that keeps clogging deserves a camera look before more cabling.

Local note

A local detail that matters for plumbing

Lindon was incorporated in 1924, and its older streets still hold farm-era homes, including the Alfred Harper House, the only Lindon property on the National Register of Historic Places. Homes from that period often have older supply lines and sewer laterals that have outlasted their design life. If your house predates the 1970s, ask for a line check before a remodel puts walls over old pipe.

Code

Permits for plumbing work in Lindon

Plumbing work in Lindon follows the International Plumbing Code, which Utah applies statewide with amendments under Utah Code Title 15A, Chapter 3, Part 3. Whether your job needs a permit depends on its scope, so ask the Lindon City building office before a remodel or a water heater swap, and let your plumber advise.
