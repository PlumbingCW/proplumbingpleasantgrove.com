Neighborhoods inside Pleasant Grove

Pleasant Grove itself has three neighborhood pages: Manila in the far north, Downtown and Midtown around Historic Main Street, and Battle Creek and Grove Creek on the east bench.

Towns we dispatch to

Lindon is the next town south, an easy run down State Street. Its homes sit beside a real commercial and industrial base, so calls range from water heaters to shop drains.

American Fork lies just north of Pleasant Grove and shares the Mount Timpanogos Utah Temple area on the border. Much of its distribution piping is over 50 years old according to the city's own 2024 conservation plan, which matters when a main fails.

Cedar Hills climbs the bench to the northeast and shares ZIP code 84062 with Pleasant Grove. Hillside homes there deal with pressure and drainage differently from valley floor houses.

Highland is the larger-lot community north of American Fork, supplied by five city wells according to its 2022 water quality report. Bigger yards mean more irrigation, hose bib and outdoor line calls.

Each town page lists its ZIP codes, the utilities that serve it and the plumbing calls that show up most. Pick the page for your street, and call the line if you are not sure which fits.

We also pick up calls in Orem and Mapleton when a plumber is nearby. Those towns do not have their own pages yet, so call the line and the dispatcher will tell you whether a plumber can reach you.

Not sure your address is covered?

Give the dispatcher your street and the problem, and you get an honest answer on the spot.
