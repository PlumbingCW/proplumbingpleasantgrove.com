The area

Two creeks, one stretch of the east bench

Pleasant Grove was first named Battle Creek, and its present name comes from a grove of cottonwood trees between Battle Creek and Grove Creek. Both creeks come down from canyons above the city. Battle Creek Canyon holds a waterfall of about 50 feet, and a monument to an 1849 clash stands at Kiwanis Park near the canyon mouth.

Real estate sites list Battle Creek and Grove Creek as neighborhoods on the east bench. Their listings show addresses such as 1100 East to 1500 East, with Hill Park and the Murdock Trail nearby. Those sites place the two areas next to each other and their street lists overlap, so we cover them together and do not claim a precise line between them.

Listings in this stretch include ranch-style houses, newer construction and larger lots. The city's Pleasant Grove homepage covers the rest of town.

Water pressure

Pressure, regulators and the bench

The city says low or high water pressure is often a home pressure regulator and that a plumber should check it. A bench home sits higher than one near Main Street, which is one reason to ask about the regulator, but the city does not publish pressure data by street.

A regulator that has failed can show up as noisy pipes, a dripping relief valve or fixtures that spit. If you see those signs, a plumber can put a gauge on a hose bib and tell you the number. See water heater repair if the relief valve is dripping.

Outdoor lines

Outdoor lines near the foothills

These checks suit any home with a yard, and the irrigation dates come from the city.

  • Pressurized irrigation is untreated water for outdoor use only and it is not connected to indoor plumbing.
  • The city fills the system gradually from mid-April and asks residents not to turn it on early, since early filling can break lines in cold weather.
  • Main valves are shut on October 4, and the home side should be drained to prevent frozen pipes.
  • A hose bib that drips all winter or a wet spot near the foundation is worth a look. See leak detection.
  • If a line from the meter to the house is the problem, see water line repair.
Water heaters

When a tank makes sense and when to ask about the rest

The city lists Battlecreek Well water at 14.6 grains per gallon for the area east of 500 East and south of 1800 North. That is about 250 mg/L, which the USGS classes as very hard, so it is a fair question for any water heater.
