About Pleasant Grove Pro Plumbing

A 24/7 plumbing line for Pleasant Grove, UT, built on three promises: a person answers, the price comes before the work, and the plumber who arrives is licensed and insured.

Who we are

A local line for plumbing problems

Pleasant Grove Pro Plumbing answers plumbing calls for Pleasant Grove and the towns around it, then sends a licensed and insured plumber to the address. The business address is 2100 W Pleasant Grove Blvd in Pleasant Grove, UT 84062, and the phone is the same number at every hour.

We keep the model simple. You call, you describe the problem, you hear a clear price before work starts, and you decide. There are no web forms to fill out and no email queue to wait in.

How to check any plumber

Verify the licence before you let anyone in

Utah licenses plumbers through the Division of Professional Licensing, and the state runs a public lookup at secure.utah.gov. It works for us and for every other company you might call.

  • Ask for the plumber's full name and licence classification before the visit.
  • Search the name at the state licence lookup on your phone while the plumber is still in the driveway.
  • Ask for the price in writing or by text before work begins, and keep it with your records.
  • Confirm what is covered by any workmanship warranty before you pay, and ask how to reach the company after hours.
What we will not do

Honest limits

We do not make health claims about your tap water. If you are worried about lead or minerals, the city publishes an annual water quality report and your physician is the right person for health questions.

We also do not treat a gas smell as a plumbing call. If you smell gas, leave the area, then call 911 and Enbridge Gas Utah at 1-800-767-1689 from a safe place before you call us.

Why local

Plumbing that fits Pleasant Grove homes

Pleasant Grove is called the City of Trees, and its older streets near the historic downtown carry mature roots over clay and cast iron sewer laterals. Newer neighborhoods on the bench have PEX and PVC, and homes near the mouth of Battle Creek Canyon near Kiwanis Park deal with spring runoff that other parts of town never see. Each of these shapes the calls we get.

Because the city sits at the foot of the Wasatch Range, winter freezes and spring melt drive many calls. A dispatcher who knows the area asks better questions about the garage, the crawl space and the sump pit, and that saves time when minutes matter.

Before we arrive

Simple steps that limit damage while you wait

You do not need a toolbox to help. These steps work for most urgent calls and take only a few minutes.

  • Find the main water valve and close it if water is flowing where it should not.
  • Stop using sinks, tubs and toilets if drains are backing up.
  • Turn off power to a wet room at the breaker only if the panel is dry.
  • Move rugs, boxes and electronics out of the wet area and photograph the damage.
  • If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 before anything else.
Our standards

What you can expect from every visit

A plumber should explain what is wrong in plain words, show you the failed part or the camera footage when there is one and give you more than one option when more than one exists. You should never feel rushed to say yes, and a good plumber is comfortable with questions.

We also tell you what we will not do. If a problem belongs to another trade, such as a foundation contractor for a bowed wall or an electrician for a missing outlet, we say so and leave it there. That honesty is part of the price you pay for an honest quote.
